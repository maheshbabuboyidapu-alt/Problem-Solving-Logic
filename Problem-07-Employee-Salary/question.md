# Problem 07 — Employee Salary Calculator

## Problem Statement

Calculate an employee's final salary using experience, performance, attendance, and tax rules.

## Rules

### Base salary
- 0–2 years: ₹25,000
- 3–5 years: ₹40,000
- 6–10 years: ₹60,000
- 11+ years: ₹90,000

### Performance bonus
- Poor: 0%
- Average: 5%
- Good: 10%
- Excellent: 20%

### Attendance penalty
- Below 70%: 20%
- 70–84%: 10%
- 85–94%: 5%
- 95–100%: 0%

### Tax
- Up to ₹30,000: 0%
- ₹30,001–₹60,000: 10%
- ₹60,001–₹1,00,000: 20%
- Above ₹1,00,000: 30%

## Input

Enter years of experience, performance rating, and attendance percentage.
