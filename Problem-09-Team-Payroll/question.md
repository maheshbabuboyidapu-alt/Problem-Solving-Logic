# Problem 09 — Team Payroll Summary

## Problem Statement

Process the payroll for a team of employees and generate a summary report.

## Salary Rules

- Up to 40 hours: normal hourly rate
- Hours beyond 40: 1.5× normal hourly rate

## Report Requirements

Calculate and report:

- Individual salary for every employee
- Total team payroll
- Average pay
- Highest-paid employee
- Number of employees who worked overtime

## Input/Data

Use a collection of employee records containing name, hours worked, and hourly rate.

## Constraints

- Overtime is exactly 1.5× the normal rate.
- Only hours beyond 40 are overtime.
- Handle an empty employee list safely.
